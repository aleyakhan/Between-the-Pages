My Featured Series: Boys of Tommen

Boys of Tommen is one of my favorite book series because of its emotional characters, relationships, and storylines. The series follows different characters, through dual-pov, as they develop their relationships, friendship, family, love, and difficult situations. I especially enjoy how the characters and their stories connect throughout the series.
